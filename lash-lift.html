Lash lift vs. lash extensions

A lash lift curls your existing natural lashes (like a perm) — no lashes are added; it lasts 6–8 weeks. Lash extensions add individual synthetic lashes to each natural lash for length and volume; they require fills every 2–3 weeks. Both are offered at Beauty Sleep Studio in Portland.

How to prepare

Come without eye makeup on and wash your lashes with an oil-free cleanser if possible. One is also provided at the studio.
