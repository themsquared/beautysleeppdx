Lash extensions are individual synthetic lashes applied one-by-one to your natural lashes. They add length, fullness, and curl so you can skip mascara and strip lashes. At Beauty Sleep Studio in Nob Hill, every set is customized to your unique eye shape and desired look — from a natural everyday look to full dramatic glam.

Classic, hybrid, and volume

Classic applies one extension per natural lash for a natural, everyday look. Hybrid mixes classic and volume techniques for added drama without going full glam. Volume applies up to 7 lightweight extensions per natural lash for a full, fluffy look. Haley customizes length and curl to your eye shape.

What to expect

Arrive with clean, makeup-free eyes. Haley will consult on your natural lash strength, eye shape, and desired look before selecting the right style, length, and curl. You lie down on a treatment table with a blanket while she works. Before you leave, she walks through aftercare: avoid water for 24 hours, skip oil-based products near your eyes, and brush daily with a clean spoolie.

Adhesive

Beauty Sleep Studio uses traditional cyanoacrylate adhesive rather than LED/UV-cured systems. Each extension is bonded to a single natural lash (not the skin) with professional-grade adhesive.

FAQ

How long do lash extensions last?

Lash extensions last through the natural lash cycle — typically 2–3 weeks before a fill is needed.

What's the difference between Classic, Hybrid, and Volume?

Classic: One extension per natural lash — a natural, everyday look ($180). Hybrid: A mix of classic and volume for added drama without going full glam ($200). Volume: Up to 7 lightweight extensions per natural lash for a full, fluffy look ($250).

How should I prepare for my appointment?

Come without eye makeup on and wash your lashes with an oil-free cleanser if possible. One is also provided at the studio.

Do I need a lash fill or full set?

If you're new to extensions or have let them grow out completely, you need a full set ($180–$250). If you still have 40–50% of your extensions, a fill ($80–$120) will restore fullness. Most Portland clients book fills every 2–3 weeks.

Are lash extensions safe?

Yes. When applied by a licensed professional like Haley at Beauty Sleep Studio, lash extensions are safe. Each extension is bonded to a single natural lash (not the skin) with professional-grade adhesive.
